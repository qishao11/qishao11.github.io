Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Control Science and Engineering at Harbin Engineering University, advised by Prof. Liang Li and working with Prof. Hong Liang. I am currently a jointly trained Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science and Informatics, University of Liverpool, visiting the Physical Intelligence Lab in the Digital Innovation Facility and working with Dr. Baoru Huang and Prof. Anh Nguyen. I received my B.Eng. degree from the College of Intelligent Systems Science and Engineering, Harbin Engineering University.

My research focuses on 3D scene representation and SLAM for multi-robot systems and embodied AI. I study how robots build shared spatial memory by connecting geometric mapping, 3D Gaussian Splatting, semantic scene understanding, and foundation-model priors for navigation, manipulation, and collaboration.
