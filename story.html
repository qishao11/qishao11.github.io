My Story

Milestones

2019

The assessment that set the direction

I sat the independent admission assessment at Harbin Institute of Technology for the engineering experimental class in Computer Science and Electronic Communication. It was my first assessment outside the classroom, and the first time I chose a direction rather than being assigned one.

The result page on the admission platform, 2019

2019 – 2023

Underwater robots taught me control

Four years at Harbin Engineering University, most of them in the underwater robotics lab. I built an eight-thruster AUV around inertial, hydrophone and stereo sensing, with real-time thrust allocation that raised transit efficiency by 25% and an RNN-based global planner that improved dynamic obstacle avoidance by 31%. From there came navigation work for the CSSC Systems Engineering Research Institute, two software copyrights, and the habit of testing an idea on real hardware.

2023 – 2024

A rice production line, and a national first prize twice

I led the scheme design, algorithms and on-site commissioning of a cloud–edge–device system on Ascend 910/310 that auto-tunes processing parameters across a rice production line — tripling throughput and letting lines in different places be adjusted in real time. The follow-up was online rice quality inspection by multi-view feature fusion, reaching 98.5% segmentation accuracy at 200 ms. Each won a national first prize at the "Challenge Cup" Open Call Special Track, in 2023 and again in 2024.

2023 – 2025

Ph.D., and the move to spatial memory

Admitted to the direct-entry Ph.D. program by recommendation, ranked 1 of 11, in Control Science and Engineering. My first paper was accepted at ICMA the same year. Since then the work has moved from production lines to 3D scene representation and SLAM — how robots build a shared spatial memory of a place.

2025

Liverpool

Joint Ph.D. training in the Department of Computer Science, University of Liverpool. A different country, and a different way of asking research questions.
